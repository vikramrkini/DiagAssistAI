# Physiotherapy Musculoskeletal Screening (Synthetic Reference)

Physiotherapy decision support should focus on functional assessment and red-flag screening.

- Red flags: progressive neurologic deficits, saddle anesthesia, bowel/bladder dysfunction, severe trauma, or unexplained weight loss with pain.
- Follow-up: aggravating/easing factors, range of motion limitations, load tolerance, and neurologic symptoms.
- Imaging or specialist referral is considered when red flags or severe progression are present.

Recommendations are supportive and do not replace clinician judgment.
