# General Outpatient Respiratory Intake Guidance (Synthetic Reference)

Use conservative triage when fever and respiratory symptoms are present.

- Red flags include respiratory distress, oxygen saturation decline, chest pain, confusion, or inability to hydrate.
- Follow-up should clarify duration, fever trend, sick contacts, and symptom trajectory.
- Suggested first-line tests in worsening scenarios may include pulse oximetry, CBC, and targeted rapid tests.

Decision support should remain non-definitive and require clinician confirmation.
