# Pediatric Fever Assessment Principles (Synthetic Reference)

Age-sensitive triage is required.

- Red flags: lethargy, poor feeding, reduced urine output, respiratory distress, persistent high fever, or concerning behavior change.
- Follow-up should include hydration status, urine output, work of breathing, and immunization context.
- Conservative escalation is advised when red flags are present.

All outputs must be reviewed by a licensed clinician.
