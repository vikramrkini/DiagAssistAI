# Dermatology Rash Triage (Synthetic Reference)

Rash-related decision support should identify emergency signs while narrowing likely causes.

- Red flags: mucosal involvement, rapidly spreading painful rash, systemic symptoms, suspected severe drug reactions.
- Follow-up: onset, distribution, morphology, exposures, medication changes, associated fever/pruritus/pain.
- Suggested investigations depend on pattern and severity; biopsy for atypical persistent lesions.

Use this as educational support only.
